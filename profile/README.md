## Hi there 👋

Welcome to **XINTEL Labs** (`@xintel-labs`) — the home of **Distributed Grid Intelligence (DGI)**. 

### ⚡ What We Are All About
We are building an open-source, sovereign framework for decentralized energy management, Virtual Power Plants (VPPs), and microgrids. Our architecture bridges Odoo ERP backends, Holochain/Adam edge nodes, and the Unyt micro-payment engine to give businesses and homeowners absolute freedom of choice over their energy infrastructure—without cloud lock-in.
